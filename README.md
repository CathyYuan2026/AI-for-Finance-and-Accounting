→ Review & Validation

→ Trial Balance  
→ Financial Statements  
→ Professional Review

## The Role of AI

AI can improve speed and efficiency in repetitive finance and accounting tasks, but generated results should not automatically be treated as correct.

Common areas requiring professional judgment include:

- Business expense vs. personal expense
- Operating expense vs. capital asset
- Shareholder transactions
- Unusual or incomplete transactions
- Tax treatment
- Missing or conflicting information

## Professional Approach

My approach is to use AI as an analytical and productivity tool rather than as a replacement for professional judgment.

A strong AI-enabled finance process should:

- Gather sufficient information before reaching a conclusion
- Identify uncertainty and missing information
- Validate outputs against source information
- Maintain appropriate review and approval controls
- Escalate matters requiring professional judgment
- Keep humans accountable for final financial decisions
